Three directions out of the home forest, each a journey rather than a page
swap, and on every page the forest morphs into forms that tell what the page
is about.

- **Музика** (right): the camera walks right along the forest; a stump grows
  into a record player, after the back cover of Jethro Tull's «Songs from
  the Wood».
- **About** (down): the story of the band and its members. The horizon
  rises and leaves a thin white strip with the mark in the middle; the trees
  shrink under it. (Roots were drawn three ways on 2026-10-20, as thin
  vessels, as fans and as a branching root system, and the user dropped
  them all: nothing read as roots at that size.)
- **Знахідки** (left): everyone's other work: photos, drawings, poems. The
  camera walks left and the trees turn into leaves, like a herbarium.

Decisions (2026-10-20, with the user): names «Music», «About»,
«Finds». Transitions were first a journey (camera sliding along a forest
three screens wide); the user then asked for morphs in place instead: music
draws in to the forest floor (mushrooms and the stump player, the stump's
cut face being the record as on the album), finds turns the land into a
strip on the right with bare leaves and a white field on the left, about
raises the horizon to a strip about a sixth of the height, no roots drawn.
After a review in Chrome the user found the finds turn dull and asked for a
richer morph like sbjectv's and a graphic accent in place of its glitch:
now the land rolls up left to right into the strip (trees become leaves as
the curl passes) and a pencil line traces the skyline on every page change,
fading as it goes. Then the user asked for the f to be part of the skyline
like the stump, so the morphs are natural: the f is now a tree in the line
and turns into a leaf on finds; on music it sinks into the ground and the
stump grows up on its own (the user preferred that to an f-to-stump morph).
The curl went too: the user saw the strip as a trunk, so on finds the ground
now sinks the trees and the f and then lifts the whole skyline into a trunk
with one long, wavering branch at the top reaching toward the title in the
corner, twigs and leaves on it (stretching the f alone left white gaps in
the morph; the flat line flows in whole).
